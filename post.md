## 👾 Option 3: Developer Communities (Reddit /r/reactjs /r/webdev, Indie Hackers)

**Title:** I built an interactive, texturized Next.js portfolio with GSAP, Lenis, and custom tooltips. Open source!

**Body:**

Hey everyone,

I just finished building and launching the third version of my personal portfolio. Since I specialize in full-stack engineering and AI workflow automation, I wanted to showcase both parts in a single, high-fidelity experience.

Here’s a breakdown of the implementation:

*   **Custom Preloader:** A light-themed (Alabaster) progress counter (`000` to `100`) animating a list of expertise keywords. It locks mounting until Next.js is fully ready, then slides away using GSAP coordinate translations.
*   **Momentum Scroll:** Handled via **Lenis** and synced with GSAP's **ScrollTrigger** to keep scrolling smooth and responsive across high-refresh-rate displays.
*   **Adaptive Hero Tray:**
    *   On desktop: macOS-like hover dock with spring scales and tooltips.
    *   On mobile: Automatically splits into a seamless CSS-keyframe infinite loop marquee to prevent margin issues.
*   **Textured UI:** Made use of fixed overlays (`mix-blend-multiply` with a warm `#f3e9d2` backdrop and `mix-blend-overlay` for SVG fractally-generated noise) to give the entire interface a warm, crispy feel.
*   **AI Nodes Showcase:** An interactive SVG canvas mapping autonomous agent tools (Zapier, n8n, Make, OpenAI, LangChain) with custom hover tooltips showing tool names.
